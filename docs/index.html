Follow Your Strengths

writing papers with LLMs: eight tricks — combine as you want

Menzies, Schmid, Lenarduzzi, Di Nucci, Esposito, Armenti · 2026
github.com/timm/how2rite · the paper (PDF)

“Know what you are good at. Work from those strengths. Then point them at whatever your field ranks as its hardest open problems.” — after Newell. This is not one rigid pipeline. It is eight separate tricks. Each works alone; most work better in pairs. Pick what you like. Each page is a two-minute read.

Want to know what your team is actually good at?
1. Know thyself — self-report lies. Grid the team from its own papers: an LLM reads 1,200 paper records in minutes and plays triad interviewee.

Have strengths — but where should you aim them?
2. Map team to field — score the venue's areas on the same grid as the people. Centroid, maximin, and blends nominate the topic; blind spots get priced.

Drowning in 10,000 search hits?
3. Read above the knee — filter by venue inside the query, read above the citation knee, snowball both directions, and treat the download rate as a finding.

Staring at a blank page?
4. Write to a grammar — a paper parses under a small grammar: intro, lit review, methods, threats, conclusion. Disagree? Edit the productions, regenerate the sample; the experiment costs minutes.

Does your draft smell like AI slop?
5. Un-slop the prose — keep the STE skeleton (short, active, one meaning per word); subtract the engagement tells (hooks, applause paragraphs). Ship the ban list as a linter, then rewrite the missing bridges by hand.

Want Reviewer 2's verdict before Reviewer 2 gets it?
6. Assemble critics — an LLM plays Reviewer 2 using the venue's own review criteria; a triage prompt splits findings into machine fixes and human decisions.

Afraid the machine will run away with your paper?
7. Pause and reflect — every computed step ends in a boxed call-out: the questions a reviewer should ask, and the exact knob to edit so the rest follows the human, not the machine.

Will your claims survive an audit?
8. Keep the receipts — every citation carries a clickable DOI; claims wait for their statistics (significance + effect size); the conclusion retells the intro with numbers attached; title and abstract must recode with zero flips.

Sample combos. Pick a topic: 1+2.   Survive review: 6+8.   Fix the writing: 4+5.   Stay in charge: 7 + any.   The full 14-step loop of the paper: all eight, in order.
