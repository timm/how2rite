\section{Reading In}
\label{sec:lit}

A topic chosen is a literature owed.
Our lit-review rules are few enough to fit in a box:
Figure~\ref{fig:prompt} shows the whole prompt, whose first three
lines are machine-read configuration and whose five rules drive the
pipeline.

Figure~\ref{fig:lit} shows the rules running on the area that
\S\ref{sec:map} chose.
The knee cuts 400 fuzzy search hits down to 38 that demand attention;
the subset overlap cuts differently, to the 94 papers that both wings
of the team would call their own.
Note that the two cuts disagree, and that the disagreement is
informative.
The top of the cites curve is crowded with general AI blockbusters
that mention software engineering only in passing; the overlap region
is where our actual topic lives.
Raw search lies; hence the rules, and hence the human hours are spent
only after the machine has shrunk four hundred papers to dozens.

The pipeline then ran its remaining steps.
A second knee, this time inside the 94, keeps the 15 papers of 1{,}021
citations or more; those are what the humans read first.
Figure~\ref{fig:lit2} codes them with three topic flags (trust,
productivity, human-method) and its cells argue for us: no paper
occupies all three circles, and four match no flag at all (generic AI
reports that fuzzy search let in).
Hence a hole: trustworthy productivity, studied with human methods, is
unoccupied at the top of this literature.
Around that core, backward snowballing collected 12{,}332 distinct
references (198 cited by three or more of the 94: the candidate
classics), and forward snowballing found 16{,}754 works already citing
the 94, so the area is far from empty.

The process itself yielded findings worth recording.
Full texts are scarce: in this workdir's companion pipeline run, only
22 of 54 chased PDFs (41\%) could be downloaded; abstracts come easier
(here, 83 of 94, or 88\%).
Google Scholar is closed to scripts; OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, and
arXiv answer machine queries gladly, so the pipeline builds only on
the willing.
An earlier full-text pass showed abstract-only coding missing about
half the flags; hence full text is coded above the knee.
Finally, none of this paper's authors appears anywhere in the 94: the
area chosen by our strengths is territory none of us has published in,
which is either a warning or an invitation.
